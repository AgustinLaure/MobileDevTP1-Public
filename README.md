Money Rush is a fork of the project https://github.com/RodCorralImage/MobileDevTP1-Public, featuring desktop and mobile compatibility, a singleplayer mode, and various visual upgrades. Developed in Unity as part of the Desarrollo para Dispositivos Móviles course at Image Campus.

Players control an armored truck with the objective of collecting as much money as possible in singleplayer or local multiplayer modes.

CONTROLS:

Player1 (DESKTOP):
A-D to control the vehicle
A-S-D to unload the money

Player2 (DESKTOP):
Left-Right arrows to control the vehicle
Left-Down-Right arrows to unload the money

Player1 & Player 2 (Mobile):
Rotate steer wheel to control the vehicle
Swipe left-down-right to unload the money
